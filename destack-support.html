Frequently asked questions

I swiped left but the photo is still in my library.
That is by design. Swiping left only marks a photo. Hold the trash button, confirm the system dialog, and the marked photos are deleted. iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.
I marked photos by mistake.
Tap Undo to step back one decision at a time, or tap Restore … pending to put every marked photo back in the queue. Your photos are not affected either way.
How do the album slots work?
Tap an empty slot above the buttons to assign an album (or create one). From then on, tapping that slot files the current photo in that album and moves to the next. Hold a slot to change its album or colour, or to clear it.
Destack says it needs photo access.
Open iOS Settings → Apps → Destack → Photos and choose Full Access. Destack reads photos on your device only; nothing is uploaded.
Can I start over?
Destack remembers which photos you have reviewed. Reinstalling the app clears that list and starts from the newest photo again.
What is the sponsored card?
Destack is free and shows one ad card after every five decisions. Swipe it away like any other card. Your photos are never shown to or sent to the advertiser.
How do I change the ad or tracking choices I made?
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking for the tracking permission; the Ad privacy options button in the app for the consent message (shown where your region requires it).
My old app was called PicSwipe.
Same app, new name. Version 1.1 renamed PicSwipe to Destack; your progress carries over.
