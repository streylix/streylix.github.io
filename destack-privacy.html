Destack Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-13 · Applies to Destack (formerly PicSwipe) for iOS and iPadOS, bundle id com.pictoswipe.app

Destack helps you clean your photo library by swiping. It has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. This page explains how your photos are handled, what the advertising provider receives, and the choices you have.

1. Your photos stay on your device

  • Destack asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos, videos and Live Photos one at a time. They are read directly from the Photos library on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your photos are never sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else, and they are not analysed by any online service.
  • Swiping left only marks a photo. Photos are deleted only when you hold the trash button and confirm in the system dialog; iOS then moves them to Recently Deleted, where they can be recovered for up to 30 days.
  • Adding a photo to an album writes to your own Photos library, on your device.
  • Destack keeps a local list of which photos you have reviewed, which are pending deletion, your album shortcuts, your running totals and whether you have seen the walkthrough. That list contains photo identifiers only, never the pictures themselves, and it never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Destack does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or health data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Destack is free and shows a small sponsored card in the deck after every five photo decisions. These ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect:

The ad SDK never receives your photos. Google's own description of this data is at developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure, and how Google uses data from partner apps is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data ourselves; AdMob reports only aggregate ad statistics to us.

3. Tracking and consent

After you grant photo access, Destack asks, in this order:

  1. Ad consent message (where your region requires one, such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland): agree, refuse or customise the purposes for which ad data is used.
  2. App Tracking Transparency (Apple's "Allow Destack to track your activity" prompt). If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show personalised ads and measure them across other companies' apps and websites, which Apple calls tracking. If you do not allow it, you still see the sponsored card, but ads are requested as non-personalised and the advertising identifier is not used.

To change your mind later: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Destack, and the Ad privacy options button in the app (shown where your region requires it).

4. Purchases

Destack has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Nothing in the app collects payment information.

5. Children

Destack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
