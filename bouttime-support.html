Frequently asked questions

How do I change the round, rest and warm-up lengths?
Tap the preset name at the top, then edit the preset (or create a new one). Rest can be switched off for back-to-back rounds.
The voice is not calling combinations.
Open the preset and check Drill Difficulty: Off is silent. Easy, Medium and Intense call combinations at different speeds; Custom lets you set the frequency and pick your own drills. Also check the ring/silent switch and the volume.
What do the numbers mean?
1 jab, 2 cross, 3 lead hook, 4 rear hook, 5 lead uppercut, 6 rear uppercut, 7 lead shovel, 8 rear shovel, 9 overhand. "1-2-3" means jab, cross, lead hook. The tutorial in General Settings explains this too.
How do dodge beeps work?
Turn on Dodging in the preset. Random beeps then simulate incoming punches during the round; the intensity slider sets how often they come.
The timer is not on my lock screen.
Live Activities need iOS 16.1 or later and must be allowed in iOS Settings → BoutTime → Live Activities.
I did not hear the round change while my phone was locked.
BoutTime uses notifications for round and rest changes in the background. Allow notifications in iOS Settings → BoutTime → Notifications.
I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.
Open General Settings → Premium and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID you bought with. Ads disappear immediately after the purchase is confirmed.
How do I change the ad or tracking choices I made?
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking for the tracking permission; in the app, General Settings → Privacy & Ad Settings for the consent message (shown where your region requires it).
Where are my statistics stored?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
