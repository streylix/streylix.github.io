BoutTime Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-13 · Applies to BoutTime for iOS and iPadOS (bundle id FoDiMacaroni.BoutTime)

BoutTime is a boxing round timer. It has no account system, no sign-up and no server of its own. This page explains the little data the app keeps on your device, what the advertising provider receives, and the choices you have.

1. What BoutTime itself stores

Everything the app creates stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your presets (round, rest and warm-up lengths, drill settings, dodge settings, timer font);
  • your custom drills;
  • your training history (date, preset name, duration, rounds completed, drill mode, whether the session finished);
  • your display settings and whether you have seen the tutorial;
  • a flag that records whether you bought Remove Ads.

None of this is uploaded, synced or shared with us. Deleting the app deletes it. BoutTime does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location or health data. The voice callouts are generated on your device by the system speech synthesiser.

2. Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, BoutTime schedules local notifications on your device so you hear round and rest changes while the app is in the background. The Live Activity on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island is rendered on your device. Neither involves any server.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of BoutTime shows ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner on the main screen and an occasional full-screen ad when you stop or finish a workout. To serve and measure those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect:

Google's own description of this data is at developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure, and how Google uses data from partner apps is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data ourselves; AdMob reports only aggregate ad statistics to us.

4. Tracking and consent

When you first open BoutTime it asks, in this order:

  1. Ad consent message (where your region requires one, such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland): you can agree to, refuse or customise the purposes for which ad data is used.
  2. App Tracking Transparency (Apple's "Allow BoutTime to track your activity" prompt). If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show personalised ads and to measure them across other companies' apps and websites, which Apple calls tracking. If you do not allow it, you still get ads, but they are requested as non-personalised ads and the advertising identifier is not used.

You can change your mind at any time:

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → BoutTime, to allow or deny tracking;
  • in the app, General Settings → Privacy → Privacy & Ad Settings, to reopen the consent choices (shown where your region requires it);
  • buying Remove Ads, which stops the app from requesting ads at all.

5. In-app purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. BoutTime receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase exists; it never sees your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks that confirmation with Apple and keeps a local "ads removed" flag. Use Restore Purchases in the app on any device signed into the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own terms.

6. Children

BoutTime is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
